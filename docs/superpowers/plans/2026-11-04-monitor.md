# Premium Laptop Watch Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this
> plan in the current session. Deployment and tests are already authorized.

**Goal:** Find premium Best Buy laptop offers and send private Telegram alerts
from an independent VPS job every two hours.

**Architecture:** Public-page browser collector produces explicit offer records.
An exact-model catalog provides hardware notes and analyst price targets. A
stateful runner filters, ranks, delivers, deduplicates, and records health.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12+, uv, Playwright, httpx, pytest, Ruff, cron, flock.

**Spec:** ../specs/2026-11-04-monitor-design.md

## Global Constraints

- Maximum $3,000 before tax; minimum RAM 32 GB.
- New and excellent open-box only; Best Buy seller and known availability.
- Only the verified private recipient; no secrets in git or logs.
- Two-hour VPS cron; preserve all other cron jobs and services.
- No claims of a historical low without historical evidence.

## Review Focus

- Open-box minima without condition must not become excellent offers.
- Unknown seller and availability must not become purchasable offers.
- Partial or empty source failures must appear in status and health alerts.
- Telegram timeout must not mark delivery successful or expose its token.
- Repeated cron installation must preserve other jobs and avoid duplicates.
